Game-ready striped milk carton.
MILK logo: Luckiest Guy font, printed into the base-color texture.
One mesh, one opaque material, one 1024px base-color atlas. Lettering and thick stripes are printed into the texture; no text or decal geometry.
Closed manifold mesh, applied transforms, triangulated, bottom-center origin, height 0.20 meters.
GLB embeds the texture and includes only the carton. Blend includes the packed texture and a separate preview studio collection.
Original three-carton concepts are preserved in ../milk_carton_variants.
Not placed into the game world or rebuilt into the game executable.
