# Character Creator Asset Licenses

The character creator may be used commercially. Keep this file with redistributed
source builds so the CC-BY shirt graphics retain their required attribution.

## CC0 3D accessories

- Quaternius, **Universal Base Characters (Standard)** — hair, beard, ranger hood,
  and round hood. CC0 1.0. https://quaternius.com/packs/universalbasecharacters.html
- João Baltieri, **Cartoon Capsule Pack Lite** — capsule male/female hair and cap.
  CC0 1.0. https://joaobaltieri3d.itch.io/cartoon-capsule-pack-lite
- Damin De Silva, **Low Poly Male and Female Models** — swept hair, short hair,
  ponytail, facial hair, caps, and round hat. CC0 1.0.
  https://damindudesilva1gmailcom.itch.io/low-poly-male-and-female-models-free-low-poly-3d-model

- iPoly3D, **LowPoly Glasses Pack** — ten selectable glasses and sunglasses adapted from the 29-model pack. CC0 1.0.
  https://opengameart.org/content/lowpoly-glasses-pack

The source packs' license statements were audited on 2026-08-17. Copies of the
Quaternius license are also stored beside its original modules.
