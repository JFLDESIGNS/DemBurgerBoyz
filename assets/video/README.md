# MUVYE source notes

The shipped files are Ogg Theora/Vorbis encodes made for Godot's built-in
`VideoStreamPlayer`. The two animated shorts use upgraded 360p, 23.976 FPS
encodes from their public-domain masters. Their legacy `_240p` filenames are
retained so existing game and save references remain compatible.

## The Restaurant Operator (1946)

- User-selected source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HUizl5jaRW0
- Encoded file: `restaurant_operator_240p.ogv`
- Note: the supplied YouTube upload exposes no machine-readable license. The film was registered in 1946; confirm the renewal/public-domain record before commercial distribution.

## Pastry Town Wedding (1934)

- Source and rights page: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Pastry_Town_Wedding_(1934).webm
- Encoded file: `pastry_town_wedding_240p.ogv`
- Rights: Wikimedia Commons marks this film public domain in the United States because its copyright was not renewed.

## The Fresh Vegetable Mystery (1939)

- Source: https://archive.org/details/the-fresh-vegetable-mystery-1939
- Encoded file: `the_fresh_vegetable_mystery_240p.ogv`
- Rights: the Internet Archive item is labeled with the Creative Commons Public Domain Mark 1.0.
