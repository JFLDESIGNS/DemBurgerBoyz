# Loading and truck opening

The complete twenty-second night-street walking clip plays at 1920x1080 with a 12 fps film cadence. It uses the illustrated movie street, matched character colors, peach-orange moon-colored logo, softened rotating rays, flickering signs/windows, and film wear. The preparation still comes from the final frame of this same clip, after all three characters exit left. During it, five small shader resources load asynchronously, one at a time with 150 ms spacing. No scene construction or GPU warmup runs during playback. There is no minimum preparation hold. Essential kitchen geometry, food, interactions and first-use effects finish before opening.

A representative legacy rig and up to twelve local saved customer appearances are warmed one at a time during preparation. Their immutable appearance materials and geometry are cached for reuse, while expression materials remain independent. Prepared fade materials stay referenced after their temporary actors are freed, preserving the generated shader variants for gameplay; live customers still own independent fades. Cola and fries handoff visuals are rendered beneath the loading still. The final kitchen render also exercises visible burger-burst poses and the completion light, and payment preparation includes the actual sale-bill material. This bounded preparation runs before the reveal, not as a scene-construction job during play. Additional uncached appearances can still incur a first-use cost.

The garbage, freezer and bread bin are positioned and rendered beneath the loading still before the reveal. The six-second opening locks input: the shutter rises from 0.25–3.35 seconds with a CC0 garage-door recording, the bunting falls and settles from 3.05–4.45 seconds, then the sign flips from 4.45–5.12 seconds. Wind and normal sign tweens pause during this sequence. The opening jingle ends with one Burger Pals announcement; starting gameplay does not repeat it. The application intro also announces the title only once. Optional jobs do not hold this sequence open.

Procedural audio uses worker threads with 80 ms gaps between cache entries. Spare patties are generated off-thread, then added one at a time at least 250 ms apart during gameplay, waiting while a burger handoff is active. A refill guard prevents overlapping producers. Initial cooking stock and core rendering warmups remain upfront.

Validation: tests/movie_street_loading_smoke.gd checks the full clip, matching preparation still, prewarming and decoder shutdown; tests/full_loading_flow_smoke.gd and tests/truck_opening_smoke.gd cover the wider loading flow.
